Overview

Helping people build healthier money habits with tiny, consistent wins.

Hammock is a concept for a lightweight savings app that encourages small, repeatable contributions instead of “all-or-nothing” budgeting. This case study focuses on the exploration and design of the core experience: seeing progress, feeling motivated, and taking the next step.

Problem

Traditional budgeting feels heavy. Most people just want to feel on track.

Many potential users I spoke with weren’t looking for another complex budgeting suite. They wanted something lighter: a simple way to build momentum toward a goal and feel like they were making progress even on busy weeks.

  • Existing tools were too rigid or time-consuming.
  • Progress often felt invisible until “end of month” recaps.
  • Motivation dropped quickly after the first few weeks.
Process

From lightweight interviews to focused interaction design.

I followed a lean process that fit within a tight timeline, while still grounding decisions in real behavior:

  • Ran quick discovery interviews to understand current saving habits.
  • Mapped a minimal happy path: create a goal, fund it, see progress.
  • Explored several visual directions for the “hero” progress moment.
  • Prototyped the core flows in Figma and ran usability walkthroughs.
Outcome

A focused experience that rewards tiny wins instead of perfect behavior.

The final concept emphasizes clarity and emotional payoff: users can see their progress at a glance and are nudged toward the next small step instead of a long list of tasks. Future iterations would focus on deeper automation and hooks into real banking data.
